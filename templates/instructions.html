A step-by-step guide

How to Use the Portal

Follow these steps to explore the available services and understand how the demonstration portal works.

01

Prepare Your Property Details

Keep your sample document number, registration year, district, mandal, village and survey number ready before starting a search.

02

Search Property Information

Open Property Search and enter the requested property details. Select the relevant location and submit the form to view the demonstration result.

03

Explore the Property Map

Use the illustrative map interface to explore the displayed location and map layers. Any shown boundaries are sample visualisations, not surveyed property boundaries.

04

Submit an EC Request

Visit the EC Request section and enter either document-based or property-based sample information to generate a demonstration request reference.

05

Request Document Verification

Enter the document number, registration year and district to save a verification request in the project's local database.

06

Review Your Activity

Open History to review saved property searches. Your Profile section displays the account details provided during registration.

Before you begin

Use sample information while exploring the academic demonstration. Do not treat generated results as certified property records.

Important Project Disclaimer TERRITY is an independent academic demonstration and is not affiliated with or operated by any government department. It does not connect to official land-registration databases or independently verify ownership, document authenticity, legal validity or surveyed property boundaries. All generated records and map illustrations are for demonstration purposes only.
